# Acoustic piano source and attribution

This file contains the compressed acoustic piano sample sprite used by Boomwhacker's piano melody and automatic piano accompaniment.

**Audio samples license:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- Asset: `mats-helgesson-piano.mp3`
- Source: [musictheory/piano.js/piano.mp3](https://github.com/musictheory/piano.js/blob/master/piano.mp3)
- Transformations: Original compressed sprite is reused with timed slices and playback-rate pitch shifting; note scheduling, gain envelopes, music and backing parts are new. No alterations are made to the underlying MP3 file.
- Credit and license apply to the piano recordings; this does not imply endorsement by the original creators.
- The authors request that the *raw recording samples* not be sold as a commercial sample library. This website uses the audio strictly to play songs/educational music, not as a sound library product.
- Audio fetch is same-origin and is not sent to a third-party synthesis service.
